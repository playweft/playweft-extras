How to play

🐶

Find a home for every pup.

Three simple rules

Rows & columns

One pup in every row and column.

Colored regions

One pup in every colored region.

No touching

Keep pups apart, even diagonally.

Controls

Touch & mouse

Tap or click

Toggle an × mark.

Double-tap or click twice

Place a pup.

Drag

Paint or erase marks in one stroke.

Start on an empty cell to paint, or an × to erase. A whole stroke counts as one Undo action.

Focus the game first. Your first confirm reveals the cursor; press again to place a pup. Create is the small button to the left of the touchpad.

Create + Options and Guide / PS are reserved for the platform menu.

Good to know

Three lives

A wrong pup costs one life. Marks don’t cost lives.

Undo

Undo does not restore lives. Use it to remove placed pups.

Hints

Reveal one logical step at a time.

Out of lives? Restart the same puzzle to try again. Solve it to automatically play the next one.

Solve without mistakes, marks or hints to earn a crown.Undo and restarting do not restore crown eligibility.
